      FAQ

      Interior demolition questions, answered

      Don’t see yours? Call 587.834.4444 or email info@stallioncontracting.ca.

      Do I need a permit for interior demolition in Calgary?

      Often, yes — especially for commercial spaces or work that touches structure, plumbing, electrical or fire separations. Requirements depend on the property and scope, so we confirm what your project needs during the site walk and coordinate the City of Calgary permit process for you.

      How much does interior demolition cost in Calgary?

      It depends on square footage, what’s being removed, access, disposal volumes and whether hazardous materials are present. After a free site walk we give you a clear, itemized quote — and if you’re building after, Stallion Contracting can quote the build at the same time.

      How long does a commercial strip-out take?

      Smaller units and single rooms can be days; larger commercial interiors take longer. You’ll get a schedule with your quote, and because the build is planned alongside the demo, there’s no waiting period between the two.

      What happens if there’s asbestos?

      Many older Calgary buildings need a hazardous materials assessment before demolition. We coordinate testing up front, and if anything is found it’s abated by certified professionals before our demo work proceeds.

      Do you also do exterior demolition?

      Yes. Interior demolition is our focus, but we also take on exterior work — garages, decks, additions, façades, small structures and concrete removal.

      Can you work in an occupied building or after hours?

      Yes. We regularly plan around neighbouring tenants with dust containment, protected corridors and evening or weekend scheduling where the building requires it.

      Do you haul away the debris?

      Always. Bins, haul-away and a final sweep are part of every job, and we sort metal, wood and concrete for recycling wherever possible.

      Can Stallion build my new space after the demolition?

      That’s exactly why we exist. Stallion Demolition is part of Stallion Contracting, a Calgary commercial general contractor. One site walk covers both, the build crew is lined up before demo ends, and you deal with one team from strip-out to keys.
